EB_List Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 25, 2026 · Last updated: August 25, 2026

EB_List is a locally operated listing-assistance application that helps its operator prepare eBay listing drafts, retrieve eBay category metadata, and review automotive parts compatibility data. This policy explains how EB_List handles information when an eBay user authorizes the application.

1. Information handled

  • eBay OAuth access tokens supplied after the user grants consent.
  • eBay account identifiers and seller information returned by authorized eBay APIs, when applicable.
  • Listing, category, catalog, item-specific, and vehicle compatibility information requested through eBay APIs.
  • Product titles, part numbers, listing drafts, and files entered or generated by the user.

EB_List does not request, collect, or store eBay passwords, payment-card details, or bank credentials.

2. How information is used

Information is used only to authenticate authorized API requests, generate and review listing drafts, retrieve eBay metadata and compatibility information, export seller-controlled files, troubleshoot the application, and comply with eBay requirements and applicable law. EB_List does not sell personal information or use eBay data for advertising.

3. Storage and retention

The current EB_List application runs locally on the operator's computer. Seller OAuth access tokens entered in the interface are held only in the active application session and are not intentionally written to the project files. Local application credentials and user-generated exports remain on the operator's device until the operator deletes them. Information is retained only for as long as needed for the purposes described above or as required by law.

4. Sharing and service providers

Information is transmitted to eBay only as needed to perform authorized API calls. EB_List does not disclose personal information to unrelated third parties except when required by law, to protect rights and security, or to operate infrastructure expressly chosen by the operator. This static policy page is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may process ordinary web request logs under GitHub's own privacy statement.

5. Security

Reasonable safeguards are used to restrict access to credentials and tokens. Users must keep access tokens and application secrets confidential and must not place them in public repositories, listing files, screenshots, or messages. No transmission or storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. User choices, access, and deletion

Users may decline authorization, close the local application session, remove locally stored files, or revoke EB_List access from their eBay account settings. Requests concerning access, correction, or deletion may be submitted using the contact information below. Identity verification may be required before a request is completed.

7. Children's privacy

EB_List is intended for business users and is not directed to children. The application does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the application, eBay requirements, or applicable laws change. The latest version and effective date will be posted on this page.

9. eBay relationship

EB_List is an independent application and is not endorsed by or affiliated with eBay. eBay data and services remain subject to eBay's terms, policies, and privacy notice.

EB_List 隐私政策摘要

EB_List 是在运营者电脑本地运行的 eBay 刊登辅助工具，用于生成刊登草稿、查询类目及审核汽车零件适配数据。程序不会索取或保存 eBay 登录密码、银行卡或银行账户资料。

经用户授权后，程序仅为调用 eBay API、生成和审核刊登资料、导出用户控制的文件及满足合规要求而处理 OAuth Token、eBay API 返回的账户标识、刊登、类目、Catalog 与 Compatibility 数据。程序不出售个人信息，也不将 eBay 数据用于广告。

当前版本在本地运行。界面中输入的 Seller OAuth Access Token 仅保留在当前会话中，不会主动写入项目文件。用户可以关闭程序、删除本地文件，或在 eBay 账户中撤销对 EB_List 的授权。
